# Dorito Meatballs

**Servings:** about 4 (not tested yet)
**Prep:** about 20 min (includes the 10 min rest) · **Cook:** about 25 min
**Rating:** ★★★★★★★☆☆☆ (7/10)
**Date saved:** 2026-10-16

## Ingredients
- Ground meat, shaped into meatballs
- Salt and pepper
- Crushed Doritos (Jalapeño Cheddar was used; try a zestier flavour)
- Bacon grease, for searing
- Manwich
- Tomato sauce
- Death sauce or another hot sauce, to taste
- Spaghetti

## Steps
1. Form the meatballs, season them with salt and pepper, and let them rest 10 minutes.
2. Press the crushed Doritos into the surface.
3. Sear in bacon grease in a cast iron pan, 1 minute per side. You don't need to preheat the pan.
4. Bake at 400°F for 15 minutes, flipping halfway, and pull them when they're 160°F inside.
5. Meanwhile, simmer the Manwich, tomato sauce and hot sauce in a separate pot.
6. Start the spaghetti right after you flip the meatballs.
7. Put the meatballs back in the oven for 5 minutes to re-crisp.
8. Ladle the sauce over at serving. Never put it in the cast iron, so the crust stays crunchy.

## Notes and variations
- The first try was 7/10, with a crunchy crust and properly cooked inside.
- Next time, bake 15 minutes instead of 20, add more spice, and maybe try a zestier Doritos flavour.
- Wipe the cast iron and put it back in the warm oven to keep it seasoned.
