# Dorito Meatballs

**Servings:** about 4 (1.2 lb of beef)
**Prep:** about 20 min (includes the 10 min rest) · **Cook:** about 20 min
**Rating:** ★★★★★★★★☆☆ (8/10)
**Date saved:** 2026-10-16 · **Updated:** 2026-10-19 (scaled from 1 lb to 1.2 lb, milk swapped for heavy cream)

## Ingredients (scaled for 1.2 lb)
- 1.2 lb ground beef
- 1 large egg
- 3 tbsp heavy cream (replaces the milk)
- 2/3 cup crushed Doritos or pickle chips, mixed into the meat
- 1 1/4 cups crushed Doritos or pickle chips, for the coating (Jalapeño Cheddar was used before; try a zestier flavour)
- 1 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- Bacon grease, for searing
- 1 can (15 oz) Manwich
- 1 cup tomato sauce
- Death sauce or another hot sauce, to taste
- Spaghetti

*The amounts for the egg, chips, salt, pepper and sauce are estimates scaled from a typical 1 lb batch. Adjust them after you cook it.*

## Steps
Everything except the spaghetti is cooked in one cast iron pan. You sear and finish the meatballs in that same pan.

1. Mix the beef, egg, heavy cream, the mix-in chip crumbs, salt and pepper. If the mix feels too wet to hold its shape, add a bit more crushed chips. Form the meatballs and let them rest 10 minutes.
2. Press the coating crumbs into the surface.
3. Sear in bacon grease in the cast iron pan, 1 minute per side. You don't need to preheat the pan.
4. Put the same pan in the oven at 400°F for 15 minutes, flipping halfway, and pull them when they're 160°F inside.
5. Meanwhile, simmer the Manwich, tomato sauce and hot sauce in a separate pot.
6. Start the spaghetti right after you flip the meatballs.
7. Put the meatballs back in the oven for 5 minutes to re-crisp.
8. Ladle the sauce over at serving. Never put it in the cast iron, so the crust stays crunchy.

## Notes and variations
- The first try was 7/10; the second try (1.2 lb, heavy cream, Lay's in the mix) was 8/10, with a crunchy crust and properly cooked inside.
- Next time, bake 15 minutes instead of 20, add more spice, and maybe try a zestier Doritos flavour.
- Heavy cream replaces the milk, which makes the meatballs richer and more tender.
- Wipe the cast iron and put it back in the warm oven to keep it seasoned.
