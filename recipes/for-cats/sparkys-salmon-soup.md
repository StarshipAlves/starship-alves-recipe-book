# Sparky's Salmon Soup (v1)

*For cats. This is a treat or a topper, not a complete meal.*

**Servings:** about 8 to 10 small portions of 1 to 2 tbsp each
**Prep:** 5 min · **Cook:** about 5 min, plus cooling
**Rating:** not rated yet (Sparky hasn't tried it)
**Date saved:** 2026-10-19

## Ingredients
- 1 small can (about 6 oz) of salmon packed in water, labelled "no salt added"
- About 2 cups water

## Steps
1. Drain the salmon and rinse it well.
2. Pick out the skin and the larger bones.
3. Simmer the salmon in the water for about 5 minutes.
4. Mash a spoonful or two of the fish back into the broth to make it fragrant.
5. Let it cool to room temperature before serving.

## Serving and storage
- Serve 1 to 2 tbsp at a time, either on its own for licking or poured over her regular food.
- Keeps 3 days in the fridge. You can also freeze it in an ice cube tray for single portions.

## Restrictions
- Use only canned salmon packed in water with no salt added. Salmon in brine, oil, or regular salted water is out.
- Never add salt, onion, garlic, or any seasoning. Onion and garlic are toxic to cats.
- Remove the skin and larger bones, because canned bones are soft but can still be a choking risk.
- This is a treat or topper only. Homemade food alone lacks taurine and other nutrients cats need.
- Check with the vet first if she has kidney issues or another health condition.

## Notes and variations
- Plain cooked chicken or white fish works the same way.
