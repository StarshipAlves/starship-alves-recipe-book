# Cast Iron Cheddar Eggs

★★★★★★★★☆☆ (8/10)

**Servings:** 6–8 wedges
**Prep time:** 5 min
**Cook time:** 25 min (15 min bake + 10 min broil)
**Date saved:** Oct 22, 2026

## Ingredients
- 9 eggs
- 3 tbsp heavy cream
- Scant 1/2 tsp salt
- Pinch of pepper
- About 1 cup shredded cheddar
- Butter or bacon grease for the pan

## Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Grease a 12" cast iron pan well, including the sides.
3. Whisk the eggs, cream, salt and pepper until smooth.
4. Pour into the pan, stir in about 3/4 of the cheddar, and sprinkle the rest on top.
5. Bake at 375°F for 15 min.
6. Switch to high broil and broil for 10 min to finish and brown the top.
7. Rest 5 min, then cut into wedges.

## Notes & Variations
- Grease the sides of the pan too, not just the bottom, so it releases cleanly.
- First cook on Oct 22, 2026 came out excellent.
- Muffin tin version: see Egg Bites.
- Add-in ideas: bacon, ham, peppers, onion, spinach, or a few dashes of death sauce.
