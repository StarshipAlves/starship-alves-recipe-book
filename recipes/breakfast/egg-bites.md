# Egg Bites

★★★★★☆☆☆☆☆ (5/10, not cooked yet)

**Servings:** 12 bites (one muffin tray)
**Prep time:** 10 min
**Cook time:** 20–25 min
**Date saved:** Oct 20, 2026

## Ingredients
- 10 eggs
- 1/4 cup heavy cream (or milk)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- About 1 cup mix-ins: cooked bacon, ham, sautéed bell peppers, onion, and/or spinach
- Butter or bacon grease for greasing the tray

## Steps
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a 12-cup muffin tray well.
2. Whisk (or blend for a fluffier texture) the eggs, cream, salt, and pepper until smooth.
3. Divide the mix-ins and cheese among the cups, then pour the egg over them until each cup is about 3/4 full.
4. Bake 20–25 min, until the centres are set.
5. Cool 5 min, run a knife around the edges, and pop them out.

## Notes & Variations
- Dollar store trays can stick, so grease them generously.
- They keep 4 days in the fridge or can be frozen. Reheat in the microwave for 45–60 seconds.
- Spicy version: add a few dashes of death sauce to the egg mix.
- Made to use up extra eggs (Oct 20, 2026).
